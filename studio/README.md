# Susanoo Reel Kit · studio

The engine room of the kit. You talk to your AI; it works in here.

**What it makes:** finished 1080×1920 reels from a single prompt. It writes the script or uses yours, records the voice, builds and animates every shot, reviews its own frames and exports an mp4. Or it edits a video of you talking.

| Mode | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `print` | tips, tools, lists and takes in a riso-print look (engraved shading, one accent ink), with a sticker presenter you can make look like you |
| `depth` | 3D explainers of what goes on inside things, in lit rooms with Bix the boxy host |
| `talking` | your own face take, cut and captioned with graphics on top |

- **Your brand everywhere:** four themes (studio, harbor, ochre, ink) or any accent colour, plus your handle.
- **Any language:** fonts for other scripts download when needed.
- **Checks before you see it:** a motion check and a craft check reject reels that are too still or too generic.
- **Runs on your machine:** no extra subscriptions beyond your AI plan and ElevenLabs.

**Start:** `SETUP.md`. Then ask *"Make a reel about …"* or *"Edit my talking head"*.

**Map:**

| Folder / file | What it is |
|---|---|
| `KIT.md` | the workflow your AI follows, start to finish |
| `playbook/` | the craft that applies to every mode: writing, staging, ideas, self-review, host, delivery |
| `modes/<mode>/` | one self-contained mode each (print, depth, talking): its GUIDE.md, runtime, parts, starter and example shots (print 16 scenes + 6 prop studies, depth 28 shots) |
| `tools/` | voice, render, checks, mixing, export |
